How it works

A retrieval-first pipeline — the model only speaks from what it can point to.

1

Describe your product

e.g. "I manufacture an electric household appliance. What BIS requirements apply?" You don't need to know the exact standard number.

2

AI understands the query

The system determines intent — product identification, certification query, testing requirement, or document search.

3

Retrieve relevant BIS evidence

Document → text extraction → chunking → TF-IDF retrieval → relevant evidence, pulled from the indexed knowledge base.

4

NVIDIA NIM generates the explanation

Retrieved evidence is passed to an NIM-powered LLM instructed to answer only from that evidence — never inventing standards, clauses, or dates.

5

Evidence shown with the answer

You see the standard, document, section, page, evidence text, confidence level, and a compliance roadmap.

6

Take action

Move toward the applicable standard, required testing, certification process, and a laboratory that can help.
